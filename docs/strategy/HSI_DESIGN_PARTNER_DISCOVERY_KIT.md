# HSI translational research design-partner discovery kit

Proposal for founder review and real discovery work. No interviews, customers, signed partner terms or willingness-to-pay results are claimed.

## Flagship workflow and value hypothesis

HSI helps translational immunology teams turn an immune-mechanism question into a transparent, source-linked, human-reviewable research brief. The testable value hypothesis is **less time to an inspectable brief with equal or better evidence/context quality**, compared with the team's existing workflow.

Initial scientific programme: RA/anti-TNF mechanisms and response heterogeneity, using authorised non-identifying research sources. SSc is an existing bounded implementation/evaluation context, not an automatically equivalent disease mechanism. Gut, brain, diabetes, patient interpretation, omics prediction and real eligibility screening are outside the first scientific release. The 20-condition lab remains a labelled software test surface.

## Thirty-minute interview: past behavior first

Obtain agreement to take notes. Use a published or de-identified research example; do not request patient records, secrets or unpublished confidential details. Recording requires separate explicit consent. Participation is not product endorsement.

| Time | Questions and probes |
| --- | --- |
| 0–3 min | Role, team and last completed investigation. “What did you have to deliver, and to whom?” |
| 3–10 min | “Walk me through the last question, step by step.” Which exact sources/tools were used, where notes lived and what was checked manually? |
| 10–16 min | “Where did you lose time?” Ask for a concrete example of conflicting findings, missing tissue/species/assay context, citation problems and hand-offs. |
| 16–21 min | “Who reviewed it? What changed after review?” Explore rejection, corrections, audit needs and what would make them distrust a tool. |
| 21–26 min | “How often does this work recur? What do you pay for now?” Identify budget owner, procurement, security/data constraints and alternatives. |
| 26–30 min | “What would a useful trial need to demonstrate?” Ask whether they could bring a permitted question and review a bounded brief. Request later follow-up permission separately. |

Avoid “Would you use our AI?” and leading claims about intelligence, speed or accuracy. Capture observed behavior separately from promises and interviewer interpretation.

### Interview note template

- Participant code, date, role and organisation category; names/contact details stored separately under approved access.
- Note-taking/recording consent and restrictions.
- Last task, frequency, sources, steps, elapsed/active time, output and reviewer.
- Specific failure/context/conflict example and current workaround.
- Buyer/procurement/data constraints, actual existing spend if voluntarily shared.
- Value signal, rejection signal, permitted pilot question and unanswered questions.
- Evidence confidence: direct past behavior / estimate / opinion / hypothetical.
- Follow-up permission: unknown until explicitly given.

## Six-week design-partner proposal

Status: **proposed; no participant enrolled or contract signed**. Target 3–5 eligible researchers/teams with a repeated task and reviewer capacity.

| Week | Activity | Evidence to retain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Agree scope/data terms, establish manual baseline and review quality rubric | Permitted question, source/release pins, baseline time and agreed rubric |
| 2 | Researcher completes a bounded HSI investigation with source inspection | Actual completion/failure, sources opened, errors and active time |
| 3 | Independent reviewer examines brief and records limitations/revision | Genuine reviewer decision and effort; no auto-promotion |
| 4 | Repeat on another scoped question; address observed friction | Repeat use, corrections, missing context and support burden |
| 5 | Repeat comparison and discuss budget/procurement | Measured task/quality comparison and actual continuation signal |
| 6 | Joint review, unresolved safety/quality issues and continuation decision | Evidence-backed recommendation; no invented adoption or ROI |

Proposed partner commitment: one 45-minute feedback discussion every two weeks plus agreed bounded tasks. Proposed offer: early access, scoped support and input into priorities. Support, processing, permitted-use, retention and publication terms require accountable agreement before a live pilot.

### Success hypotheses: not approved release thresholds

The notes suggest 3 of 5 completing an investigation, 50% weekly return, 70% brief usefulness and two continuation signals. These are discovery hypotheses. Small cohorts make percentages unstable; report raw numerator/denominator and failures.

- Completion: eligible participants completing the agreed source/review/export task / eligible participants offered that task.
- Weekly return: eligible participants with another research task in week N / eligible activated participants still in the pilot; exclude rehearsed demo clicks.
- Usefulness: defined participant rating on an agreed question/rubric, with response denominator and missing responses.
- Time: paired tasks or an agreed matched comparison; distinguish active work, waiting and reviewer time.
- Quality: expert source relevance, context preservation, opposing evidence and unsupported-claim assessment; convenience cannot compensate for safety failures.
- Continuation: actual recorded consent/intent or signed next step, not a presumed purchase.

### Pricing experiments

The supplied ranges (£50–£150 per researcher/month and £2,000–£10,000 per 6–12-week team pilot) are **unvalidated interview probes**, not published pricing or forecast revenue. Establish current spend, buyer, data/security requirements and measurable value first. Separate free public-source exploration, paid research-team workflow and later enterprise/private-source requirements.

## One-page positioning narrative

**Problem hypothesis:** search, biological context checking, conflict comparison and review fragment across tools, slowing investigations.

**Product:** HSI connects permitted evidence and biological context into inspectable briefs with visible uncertainty and accountable human review.

**Differentiation to test:** immune-domain depth, claim/passage/context lineage, opposing evidence and reproducible reviews. These are priorities, not proven competitor superiority.

**Current proof:** locally tested engineering workflow and labelled synthetic validation. Real expert-labelled scientific quality, time savings, weekly adoption and live operational acceptance remain unproven.

**Business hypothesis:** research-team subscription or scoped paid pilot after workflow value is shown.

**Next milestone:** a real team repeats the bounded investigation with approved sources and independent review, generating measured completion, quality and continuation evidence.
