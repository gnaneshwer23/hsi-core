# HSI Competitor Analysis — November 2026

**Status:** Founder strategic assessment (directional; not a quantified market-share benchmark).  
**Scope:** Public product capabilities vs intended HSI vision.  
**Honesty:** Proposed differentiators below are opportunities, not established advantages.  
**Later UK-workflow depth (3 Nov 2026):** [HSI_AKSH_ISI_COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md](./HSI_AKSH_ISI_COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md) (Aksh diabetes incumbents + Causaly/Elsevier; Owkin as Partner; RA context-mismatch wedge). Multi-year loop: [HSI_ROADMAP.md](./HSI_ROADMAP.md).

Health Super Intelligence (HSI) competes across several markets, not just one. Ambition combines biological research, clinical intelligence, longitudinal personal health, and clinical trials.

**Out of portfolio (founder 2026-11-02):** standalone scientific education / edtech (BioRender–Labster–Visible Body lane). Biology teaching may appear inside research or product UX as supporting content, but **HSI Science is not a product pillar.**

**Most important finding:** Owkin and BenchSci are particularly close to the scientific intelligence vision; Function Health is a major competitor to the broader connected-health ambition.

**Do not position HSI as the first company connecting biology, medical data, and AI.** Several well-established companies already pursue substantial parts of that vision.

---

## 1. Closest strategic competitors

### 1. Owkin — very high overlap

**Biomedical AI · Multi-omics · AI scientist**

Owkin’s K Pro coordinates specialised AI tools for biomedical discovery using multimodal patient data. Its stated vision of biological artificial superintelligence is especially close to HSI’s long-term ambition.

- Site: https://www.owkin.com/
- **HSI opportunity:** Begin with deep, transparent immunology and metabolic research workflows rather than competing immediately across all drug discovery.

### 2. BenchSci — biological knowledge graph · evidence intelligence

BenchSci’s ASCEND and EMET combine disease biology, evidence-linked relationships, and agentic scientific workflows. Pharmaceutical customer base is an important benchmark for ISI Research.

- Site: https://www.benchsci.com/
- **HSI opportunity:** Differentiation through explainable immune mechanisms, accessible research experiences, and carefully scoped clinical translation.

### 3. Function Health — personal health intelligence · biomarkers · longitudinal health

Function’s Medical Intelligence vision combines biomarkers, imaging, wearables, multi-omics, and clinical expertise for personalised insights. Significant overlap with Aksh Health and the wider HSI health-data vision.

- Sites: https://www.function.ai/ · https://www.functionhealth.com/
- **HSI opportunity:** Focus on diabetes and metabolic conditions, continuous care journeys, and clinician collaboration instead of broad preventive-health testing.

## 4. Where HSI could differentiate

Proposed differentiators — **not** established competitive advantages:

1. **Mechanism-first scientific intelligence** — exceptional depth in immune and metabolic biology before every therapeutic area.
2. **Research-to-care continuity** — scientific mechanisms, evidence, and authorised patient context with hard boundaries between research hypotheses and clinical decisions.
3. **Traceable reasoning** — citations, conflicting findings, uncertainty, graph relationships, and human approvals central to the experience.
4. **One shared intelligence foundation** — reuse governed scientific knowledge across ISI, Clinical, Aksh, and Trials without making every product identical.
5. **Focused initial market** — immunology researchers or metabolic-health clinical teams before a broad health-intelligence ecosystem.

### Recommendation

Do **not** initially compete against all six across their entire portfolios.

**Start with Immune Super Intelligence** as the strongest entry point: an evidence-grounded, mechanism-aware research workbench for immunology and metabolic disease.

Use that to establish scientific credibility, validate the knowledge graph and reasoning workflows, and attract research collaborators. Expand selectively into clinical intelligence, Aksh Health, and trials as data access, partnerships, and regulatory readiness permit.

**Positioning to test:**

> Health Super Intelligence connects biological mechanisms, scientific evidence and authorised health data to help researchers and healthcare professionals understand complex health questions—with traceable reasoning and human oversight.

Eventual advantage depends on validated scientific performance, proprietary or licensed data access, integrations, adoption, and measurable outcomes — **not** the breadth of the website alone.
