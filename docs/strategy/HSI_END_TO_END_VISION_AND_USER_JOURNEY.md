## Product promise

**Human Super Intelligence (HSI)** is becoming a connected scientific intelligence, discovery and collaboration platform.

**Goal:** Help researchers move from a scientific question to a better-informed decision, a testable experiment and the people or resources needed to advance their research.

**Not building:** another literature search engine, standalone AI chatbot, avatar application or professional networking site. Those experiences connect **through** one evidence-grounded scientific platform.

**Promise:** Understand the mechanism. Preserve the evidence. Explain the uncertainty. Inform the next decision.

## 4. Example investigation (ambition narrative)

Postdoctoral researcher: *whether microbial metabolites contribute to neuroinflammation.*

Question to the Scientific Companion:

> I have RNA-seq results showing altered inflammatory signalling. Could microbial metabolites explain this, and how can I investigate it?

Intended progression (ambition — not a live E2E claim):

1. Analyse available data — quality limits, genes, pathways, signals  
2. Retrieve relevant evidence — metabolites, immune signalling, neuroinflammation  
3. Build a mechanism map — metabolites → receptors → pathways → cellular → neurological  
4. Challenge the explanation — causality vs association vs competing mechanisms  
5. Propose experiments — perturbation, inhibition, measurements, controls (**subject to scientific review**)  
6. Discover collaboration opportunities — groups, labs, verified matching projects  
7. Produce an actionable research brief — evidence, uncertainties, hypotheses, experiments, collaborators  

The companion supports **follow-up** throughout the investigation, not one-shot answers.

## 6. Where the avatar fits

The avatar is the **human-facing interaction layer** — not the scientific reasoning engine.

Intended experience: speak naturally while HSI displays evidence, graphs, datasets and research outputs beside the conversation.

| Mode | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Text | Precise, searchable discussions |
| Voice | Hands-free scientific dialogue |
| Avatar | Optional audiovisual companion |

All three modes use the **same authorised scientific services**. Conversations preserve citations and limitations. Sensitive research data must **not** be sent to third-party avatar services without appropriate controls and permission. Nuance-style integration is a **feasibility evaluation**, not an approved production dependency.

## 8. Recommended development sequence

Phases may overlap where technically sensible. **Do not delay validating the research product to finish avatar or networking.**

| Phase | Focus | Engineering emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** Production foundation | Public acceptance where owed; research-workspace integration; security; scientific reliability; **agent durable execution / ARCH-009** | Current agent-backend units (checkpoints, fences, fingerprints); no held-migration enablement without owner |
| **2** Scientific Companion | Text-based evidence-grounded conversations; dataset discussion; critique; experiment planning | Same authorised services as workbench |
| **3** Voice and audiovisual companion | Voice + optional Nuance-style avatar evaluation | Privacy and provider gates first |
| **4** Research identity and network | Verified researcher / lab / organisation profiles | Privacy and collaboration preferences |
| **5** Opportunity intelligence | Match to verified groups, opportunities, funding, placements | Explainable matching; no invented seeking |
| **6** Connected scientific collaboration | Consent-based introductions, circles, shared rooms | Evidence-linked; permissioned sharing |
| **7** Living research intelligence | Reassess saved investigations as new permitted evidence arrives | Provenance-preserving updates |

## 9. Value loops

| Loop | Path |
| --- | --- |
| **Scientific discovery** | Question → evidence → mechanism → uncertainty → hypothesis → experiment → research output |
| **Collaboration** | Interests → relevant people/orgs → verified opportunity → introduction → collaboration → new research |
| **Learning** | Saved investigation → new evidence/results → reassessment → improved hypothesis → next decision |

Strategic opportunity: researchers progressively develop a programme with scientific context and connections preserved — not search → answer → leave.

## 10. Final vision line

**From Scientific Questions to Scientific Progress**

| Verb | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Investigate | Ask questions and explore data |
| Understand | Connect mechanisms, evidence and uncertainty |
| Discover | Generate and test better scientific ideas |
| Connect | Find people, laboratories and opportunities |
| Advance | Collaborate, reassess and progress research |

**Defining idea:** HSI should become an intelligent research environment that helps scientists understand complex biology, challenge assumptions, design better investigations and connect with people and resources that turn promising ideas into meaningful scientific progress.
