## 4. Landing-page implication (test, not overclaim)

Retain the six-domain architecture. Test a sharper hero that states problem and outcome:

- **Brand:** Human Super Intelligence  
- **Proposition:** Connect the biology. Challenge the evidence.  
- **Support:** Investigate scientific mechanisms across six domains of human biology. Compare findings in context, trace supporting evidence and mark what remains uncertain.  
- **Primary proof:** one concrete investigation (sources, context, conflict, uncertainty) — stronger than a capability list.

Live CTAs must stay delivered paths (`/welcome` research preview, `/how-it-works` for “Explore the platform”). Do not invent undelivered “beta mail”, retired `/platform` destinations, or clinical claims.

---

## 5. Potential YC application focus

YC supports early-stage biotechnology and life-science startups, including academic spin-outs. For HSI, centre an application on four truths:

1. **Scientific problem** — evidence is fragmented across domains and experimental contexts.  
2. **Unique insight** — context-aware evidence assessment (where a claim holds, fails, or remains uncertain).  
3. **Working product** — living knowledge graph + investigation loop with honesty labels.  
4. **Demand signal** — researchers who actually use the research preview / design-partner path.

Six domains explain the long-term platform. The initial validated workflow explains why the company should exist **today**.

## 6. Recommendation

Add **Undermind, Output Labs, Parallel Bio, Anto Biosciences and CellType** to high-priority competitor research.

Do **not** copy their full product strategies. Use them to sharpen HSI’s differentiation:

> A connected scientific evidence and reasoning system that helps researchers determine what biological findings actually establish, under which conditions, and with what uncertainty.
