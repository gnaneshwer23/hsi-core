# HSI product, website and delivery audit

**Audit date:** 13 November 2026. **Repository/main and latest production deployment:** `84d7b5a02ceaa82bdc099c8d45fb98d014ff2998` (PR #300 merge). **Decision:** credible public research demonstration; **not an accepted live scientific research pilot**. Research writes were observed disabled. This is an audit and proposed delivery plan, not permission to enable providers, apply held migrations, grant roles or promote scientific knowledge.

Companions: [every-route backend matrix](PAGE_BACKEND_MATRIX.md), [complete CSV](route-backend-matrix.csv), [machine-readable matrix](route-backend-matrix.json), [sanitized observation manifest](verification-evidence.json). The matrix covers **75 declared UI route entries, including six compatibility aliases, plus sitemap and robots**. Each row contains purpose, CTA, actual data/API/database/authentication status, states, maturity, recommendation, metadata and verification limitations.

## 1. Executive summary

HSI has real engineering: a deployed React research website, a versioned candidate graph, authenticated API contracts, source/reviewer gates, structured comparison, bounded evaluation/provider infrastructure, benchmark-curation machinery and durable reference ledgers. It is inaccurate to call it only an AI-generated landing page. It is equally inaccurate to describe it as a production-ready scientific decision engine. The remaining critical path is source/provider authorization, real isolated persistence and researcher/reviewer acceptance, independently adjudicated cases, and operational release evidence. [E01–E15]

The homepage now explains conflicting evidence, biological context and human governance well. Its main problem is **availability ambiguity**: visitors can interact with polished demonstrations but cannot readily determine whether they can perform the depicted real investigation today. This ambiguity culminates in “Request access,” which reaches a browser draft rather than a delivered request. Labels are present and often excellent; burying the important distinction in repeated caveats still creates a usability problem. [E04–E07]

Five release priorities:

1. **P0, public conversion:** deliver access/partnership requests to an accountable queue, or change the CTA to an action that actually occurs. Signup creates identity, not an access application. The accessibility/security notices also lack a delivered reporting route. [E06, E07]
2. **P0, egress consistency:** review or gate legacy public bibliography/abstract/trace routes and direct browser literature retrieval. These bypass the stronger research gateway's source/quota contracts. This is a verified control-boundary difference, not a claim that misuse or a rights violation has occurred. [E10, E11]
3. **P1, coherent wedge:** preserve the SSc question when leaving the homepage. Its current “Explore the Investigation” link opens a gut–neuro default. The public graph also defaults to a gut-immune question, while SSc source records are accessible in a separate mode. Align one real example around immune activation → fibroblast activation → fibrosis without inventing missing links. [E04, E08, E09]
4. **P0/P1, actual acceptance:** complete A/B and an approved production audit-store adapter; do not treat the merged reference archive as a Vercel/Neon integration. [E12–E15]
5. **P1, evidence of quality and operations:** expert panel, restricted curation workspace, final source-backed 120 cases and locked evaluation, real incident/restore/soak evidence. Preparation exists; completion does not follow from CI. [E16–E18]

Launch a clear **public demonstration and collaboration introduction** first. Invite researchers to a controlled pilot only after the independent acceptance gates pass. Keep the knowledge graph as HSI Core infrastructure that makes research reasoning inspectable, not a separate marketing product or a decorative proof of validated knowledge.

## 6. Backend-connectivity interpretation

**Static is not mocked backend.** Home/Audit/Evidence/Briefs demonstrate local interaction without pretending to execute a server investigation. The public candidate graph loads hash-verified JSON. Those are real deployed UI capabilities, but no private database connection is required. [E04, E05, E08]

**Authenticated page shell is not an authenticated data read.** The saved-workspace and scientific-review pages call real same-origin contracts; anonymous requests correctly deny. Their CI fixtures fulfil API requests. A functional UI test does not establish Neon persistence, actual provider login, pool reuse isolation or reviewer revocation in the deployed environment. [E10, E13–E15]

**Legacy source retrieval is real code, not a two-model agent run.** `scientific-pubmed` queries NCBI, `scientific-abstract` requests Europe PMC core/abstract content and `scientific-investigation-trace` returns a nonpersistent bibliographic trace. `/science/literature` also fetches Europe PMC directly on load/search. These inspected paths lack the newer source-clearance receipt and shared-admission composition, and legacy JSON parsing is not stream-size bounded. Queries in GET URLs can enter ordinary infrastructure logs; regex length/control-character checks do not establish sensitive-input screening. No provider request was made to assess current upstream availability or licence status. Gate, consolidate or explicitly authorize a separate metadata-only path; do not infer public availability means every passage action is permitted. [E11]

**The new audit store is deliberately not production composition.** SQLite reference tests demonstrate durability, immutable/idempotent decisions, revocation and integrity. The reference refuses Vercel/Lambda and is not an accepted Neon RLS/encrypted evidence vault. Production service composition does not instantiate it. Outcome receipts are freshly authorized backend integrity receipts, not independent scientific validation or signed external source clearance. [E13–E15]

**Operations code is not operational evidence.** Request controls, durable stores and worker contracts exist. The inspected production readiness URL is absent; on-call identity, actual delivered alert, shared service acceptance, restore, kill-switch drill, cost cap and fourteen-day soak remain acceptance tasks. Scientific owner is Dr. Gnaneshwer Jadav per user instruction; security and operations owners remain unassigned. [E17, E18]

## 10. Subscription, launch updates and visitor conversion

**Recommended single path:** “Register research interest” → one delivered enquiry form → receipt/confirmation → owner triage → invitation if eligible and separately granted. Include an optional interest selector (research pilot, collaboration, updates). A partner uses the same queue; an investor contact does not require a separate product track. No payment/pricing subscription platform is justified by the inspected implementation.

Launch updates can be useful while live acceptance is gated, but only if there is an actual approved email service, publication owner, cadence and unsubscribe/suppression handling. None was established in the inspected site source. Existing example digest settings are not a delivered newsletter. Do not add a frontend-only “Subscribed” confirmation. [E06, E22]

Minimum proposed implementation:

- Collect email and interest; name/institution optional. Ask for a short **nonconfidential** research topic only when needed. Do not collect PHI, source passages, unpublished studies, credentials or benchmark questions.
- Access/collaboration processing and optional product updates have separate choices; updates are unchecked by default. Use confirmation/double opt-in for updates as an operational trust/abuse measure, not as a claimed universal legal conclusion.
- Persist a stable request ID, minimal consent/policy version and timestamp. Normalize/deduplicate email+purpose; use idempotent submission/outbox. Duplicate submissions should return a neutral receipt without exposing whether a person already exists.
- Add rate limits, honeypot/time checks and bounded requests; escalate to accessible challenge only if abuse evidence warrants it. Keep provider credentials server-only.
- Deliver to an approved monitored queue. Retry with idempotency and a dead-letter path; an owner must reconcile accepted-but-undelivered requests. Confirm receipt only after durable acceptance, and distinguish pending notification from delivered response.
- Privacy content must state the actual controller/contact, purposes, processors, retention/deletion route, update choices and operational handling. Have the accountable privacy/security owner review it; current notices explicitly do not claim independent legal-policy review. Do not invent those details.
- Measure only content-free funnel events: access CTA opened, form started, validation failed, durable submission accepted, confirmation completed, notification failed and invitation offered. No raw questions, emails or source text in analytics. Audit analytics injected at hosting level separately; a source scan alone cannot prove absence of all tracking.

Dependencies: user approval of actual recipient/service/configuration, ownership, data handling and update content. This audit does not select a vendor, send invitations or subscribe anyone.

## 11. UX, content and design recommendations

The inspected homepage reads as a **credible scientific demonstration with a developing research platform**, rather than the old disconnected product catalogue. The primary hero is specific and the synthetic audit is useful. It is not yet a fully accepted premium researcher environment because the private journey, examples, access request and proof are fragmented.

Keep the restrained navy/teal scientific system, Host Grotesk typography, labelled conceptual imagery, keyboard alternatives and text-based evidence inspection. The graph now behaves as an exploratory instrument; it should remain integrated with question/context/brief, not become the sole product story. [E04, E08]

Specific improvements:

1. Put audience and current availability beside the hero. Prefer one short status sentence to repeated long caveats in every section.
2. Use one SSc example through Audit, graph and brief. Add a visible context-change notice when a link opens another example or dataset.
3. Move personal/workspace demos, clinical-style fixtures and capability laboratories out of ordinary researcher navigation. The current secondary workspace bar includes twelve destinations and maps Topic map and Knowledge graph to the same default experience. [E23]
4. On mobile, collapse optional filter groups behind a labelled controls disclosure and offer local graph/list first. Current 390px graph passes overflow/a11y checks but long filter stacks and a dense global graph create a scanning burden. This is a usability improvement, not a newly observed failing test.
5. Keep assertion-level provenance and missingness near the decision. A source pointer, green component or attractive mechanism chain must never imply cleared/verified claim support.
6. Provide an actual reporting channel. Asking someone to draft an accessibility/security report locally is honest but does not provide an operational response path.
7. Optimize hero delivery/font/render timing using the measured lab evidence; avoid replacing the scientific product demonstration with more conceptual artwork. Motion should answer selection, not obscure source/context transitions.
8. Add proof only when it exists: permitted reviewed example, dated methodology, real appointed reviewer roles, bounded benchmark failures and correction record. Do not manufacture testimonials, team affiliations or science scores.

## 15. Ninety-day product roadmap

- **Days 1–30: truthful public conversion and accepted staging foundation.** Deliver the Access path, coherent SSc demonstration, consistent egress boundary, approved per-scope persistence plan, owner appointments and private curation workflow. Keep three primary roles: orchestrator, evidence/context investigator, scientific verifier. Planning stays deterministic until tested.
- **Days 31–60: one accepted research journey and a real locked corpus.** Complete SSc plus RA/SLE under actual scoped human authority; complete permitted expert-adjudicated final cases and a separately authored development set; preregister science floors/holdout protocol. Integrate durable worker and source-revocation propagation. Publish failures and unresolved cases internally; do not tune to locked answers.
- **Days 61–90: bounded evaluation and invitation-only pilot if gates pass.** Compare configurations under equal retrieval and budget, publish denominators/intervals and hard-gate failures, complete soak/restore/alerts/release packet, then onboard a small named pilot cohort with review of every material brief and graph promotion. Collect completed-task rate, correction burden and usefulness before expanding domains or agent count.

Dates are capacity envelopes. Approval, source and SME availability are critical-path dependencies. A limited 120-case result cannot establish universal immunology or clinical safety; small subgroups/rare failures may require more cases.

## 16. Final launch recommendation and risk register

**Launch now as:** a public research demonstration of context-conditioned evidence comparison and HSI Core's inspectable candidate network, with clear research-only labels and an honest next action. Launch a delivered collaboration/access funnel only after it actually accepts and routes requests. Keep API/account availability and scientific readiness separate.

**Defer:** unrestricted live research, public expert-gold claims, general scientific approval, autonomous hypothesis/discovery claims, broad paid subscriptions, community launch without moderation, and clinical/PHI/eligibility features in this repository.

**Must not claim:** that graph proximity is biology; that a publication pointer is permitted passage use; that model agreement resolves science; that signup grants research/reviewer authority; that a local example reviewer is independent approval; that a merged PR proves database activation; that liveness/config health proves readiness; that a hundred-plus tests or 120 allocation slots establish accuracy.
