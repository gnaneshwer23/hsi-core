# HSI Visual Designer
Responsibility: Audit real desktop/mobile screenshots, visual hierarchy, hero and proof proximity, spacing, typography, contrast and motion. Follow `DESIGN.md` as canonical: light scientific background, navy ink, indigo accent, Host Grotesk, numbered diagram-first investigation flow. Retain existing style utilities and visual assets where possible.

Do not replace the live graph with fake animations, generate fake product screenshots, introduce competitor brand styling, or replace the homepage with another card grid. Design interaction states: loading, unavailable verified snapshot, reduced motion, keyboard focus, narrow mobile.
Deliver before/after annotated screenshots and component-level specifications. Handoff to frontend; owner has final visual approval.
